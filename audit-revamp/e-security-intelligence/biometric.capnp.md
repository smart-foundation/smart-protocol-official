# Audit — biometric.capnp (460 lines)

## Verdict
**FIX (targeted).** The file is honest about its scope ("Biometric hardware interaction… Auth orchestration lives in auth.capnp", `:10-11`) and its zero-cost posture passes: no bulk payloads are inline anywhere, `getEnrolled @2` is paginated (`:323`), and the `BiometricEvent` union carries a `custom @5 :Data` variant (`:426`). Three defects matter: a crypto algorithm enum frozen in a required position (a third copy in the batch), the platform-specific modality and sensor vocabularies the R5 rule names directly, and an authentication result that cannot express *why* it failed.

## Wire-breakers
None. Method ordinals @0–@13 unique (`:315-353`); `BiometricEvent` union @0–@9 with `custom @5`; struct ordinals collision-free; all `T.*` references resolve. One unused promoted type: `T.BiometricTemplateId` exists (`types.capnp:439`) and is used by `input.capnp:70,358`, but **this file** — the owner of biometric templates — uses `T.BiometricId` for template ids instead.

## Duplicates & overlaps
- `SigningAlgorithm` ↔ `crypto.capnp:610-635` `SignatureAlgorithm`, `crypto.capnp:263-296` `HashAlgorithm`, `signature.capnp:98-125`.
- `LockoutInfo` (`:110-119`) ↔ `access.capnp:397-406` `LockoutInfo` — **same name, different fields** (`remainingAttempts`/`lockoutExpiresAt`/`failedAttemptCount`/`kind` vs `startedAt`/`endsAt`/`failedAttempts`/`timed`). `LockoutKind` (`:121-127`) is byte-identical to `auth.capnp:309-315`.
- `AuthResult`/`AuthPrompt`/`AuthAttempt`/`AuthSubscription` (`:217`, `:131`, `:202`, `:355`) collide **by name** with `auth.capnp`'s `AuthResult` (`:125-133`, a union) and `AuthSubscription` (`:319-322`). Two `AuthResult` types with different shapes in one batch; a consumer cannot tell which it holds.
- `MultiModalAuth` (`:166-175`) and `BiometricAuthConfig` (`:150-163`) duplicate `AuthPrompt`'s own `allowedModalities`/`timeout`/`challenge`/max-attempts fields (`:135-147`) with no declared precedence — two knobs for one policy inside one file.
- `LivenessCheck` (`:387-396`) is returned only by the `BiometricEvent.livenessFailure @7` variant (`:423`), whose payload's `passed @0 :Bool` would presumably always be false — a consumer must check a field inside a variant named "failure".

## Gaps & holes
- No declared behaviour when `maxAttempts` is reached and the modality becomes temporarily unavailable mid-prompt (`AuthPrompt.timeout @6` and `BiometricAuthConfig.maxAttempts @5` both exist; the transition is not modelled — `BiometricEvent.lockoutStarted @0` covers the subsystem, not the in-flight prompt).
- `getTemplateCompatibility @12 (fromVersion, toVersion) -> (compatible :Bool, migrationRequired :Bool)` (`:349`) returns two booleans with no migration path and no way to perform the migration. A backend that reports `migrationRequired = true` leaves the caller with nothing to call. The template-version scheme (`TemplateVersion`, `:244-251`) exists with no declared semantics for what makes a version breaking vs compatible.
- `remove @4 (templateId)` (`:329`) has no declared behaviour when the enrolling hardware is gone: orphaned templates remain listed by `getEnrolled` with no staleness marker.

## Zero-cost & eco
Pass, recorded as praise: no inline bulk payloads (`noiseProfile @3 :Data` at `:262` is a small spectral vector, `challenge`/`signature`/`voicePhraseHash` are all small by construction). `BiometricEventSubscription.pull @0` (`:410`) is unbounded but naturally low-volume.

## UX & implementer guidance
- `AuthSubscription.pull @0 () -> (result :AuthResult, attempt :AuthAttempt)` (`:356`) — the field name `result` shadows the `AuthResult` type, so a reader must pause to see they are different things.
- `EnrollStep` (`:374-384`) emits ordinals 0,1,5,6,7,8,2,3,4 out of order. Harmless on the wire; a signal of hand-editing that invites accidental reuse.
- `BiometricAvailability` (`:95-108`) mixes *state* (`available`, `isLockedOut`) with *error* (`error @5 :T.Error`) and *counters* (`failedAttempts`, `enrolledCount`) in one struct with no discrimination, so a consumer cannot tell "no hardware" from "hardware present and locked" without checking four fields.
- `BiometricHealth.lastError @6 :Text` (`:199`) is a free-text error where a typed code would let consumers react; the file's own `HardwareErrorEvent.errorCode @1 :Text` (`:433`) is likewise Text.

## Recommended actions
1. **Move `SigningAlgorithm` out of this file**: `BiometricToken.algorithm` should carry an open token (or reference a shared crypto vocabulary once `crypto.capnp` is fixed). Gain: a 2046 signature scheme becomes verifiable instead of unverifiable; cost: one field type change plus a conformance list.
2. **Open `BiometricModality` to a `Text` token**, publishing today's spellings as `const`s. Gain: any 2046 sensor becomes requestable and reportable; `custom` stops being a black hole. Cost: consumers switch on strings; the security-relevant fact (`SecurityLevel`) stays typed.
3. **Use `T.BiometricTemplateId` for template ids and keep `T.BiometricId` for hardware.** Gain: the two namespaces stop being confusable; cost: a field type change and no ordinal movement.
4. **Give `AuthResult` an error payload and a terminator** (`result :AuthResult, attempt :AuthAttempt, done :Bool` or a union with `T.Error`). Gain: "cancelled" and "sensor died" become distinguishable, and stream end becomes detectable; cost: one field.
5. **Merge `SensorLocation` and `SensorSubtype` into one open vocabulary, or declare the split rule.** Gain: a rear camera stops being describable two ways; cost: two enums become one.
6. **Collapse `MultiModalAuth`/`BiometricAuthConfig` into `AuthPrompt`.** Gain: one policy per prompt instead of two that may disagree; cost: field moves, no ordinals lost.
7. **Declare template-migration semantics** for `getTemplateCompatibility`. Gain: `migrationRequired = true` stops being a dead end; cost: one comment plus a migration method if backends need one.
