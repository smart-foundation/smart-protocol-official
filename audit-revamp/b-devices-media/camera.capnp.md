# audit: `schema/camera.capnp` (563 lines)

## Verdict
**FIX (targeted), with one structural gap that is the most valuable single fix in this batch after audio's `submit`.** camera.capnp's payload discipline is right where it matters — `capturePhoto` and `stopRecording` return `T.DataHandle` (camera.capnp:36, 38) — and its typed ids and `T.Pagination` are correct. But it has **no frame-delivery contract at all**: the only delivery mechanisms are `startPreview @7 (cameraId, surfaceId :T.SurfaceId)` (compositor-owned) and `routeStream @24 (cameraId, route :CameraStreamRoute)` with a `consumerId :T.AppId` and no method to read frames. A headless consumer — an OCR pipeline, an AR app, a browser `getUserMedia` — cannot obtain a frame without either becoming a compositor surface or inventing a side channel; every backend already does exactly that (V4L2, AVFoundation, MMF). The second structural problem is `stopRecording @4 → (data :T.DataHandle)`, which materialises an entire recording (a 4K60 30-minute clip is ~22 GB) as one result. Everything else is ordinary: a `PhotoFormat`/`RawColorSpace` conflation, a verbatim `WatermarkRequest` duplicate with capture.capnp, a same-named `CalibrationStatus` that is an enum here and a struct in display.capnp, and no consent/authority of any kind.

## Wire-breakers
- **No compile errors, no ordinal collisions** (verified mechanically).
- `camera.capnp:469-475` — `CameraStreamRoute { streamId @0 :T.CaptureId; consumerId @1 :T.AppId; ... }` is the only "stream routing" concept, and `startStream`/a frame reader **does not exist in this file**. `capture.capnp:50` has `startStream @12 → (streamId :T.CaptureId)` and likewise has no reader. So `routeStream` points at a stream that cannot be started and frames that cannot be received.
- `camera.capnp:37-38` — `startRecording @3 → (recordingId :T.RecordingId)` then `stopRecording @4 (recordingId) → (data :T.DataHandle)`. Nothing between them returns data: a recording is unbounded in duration, and `data` is a single `T.DataHandle` whose `inline` variant (types.capnp:384) would be catastrophic if a backend ever chose it for a multi-GB clip.
- `camera.capnp:541` — `RawCaptureOptions.bitDepth @0 :UInt8` has no valid range documented; a backend that receives `0` or `64` has no defined behaviour.

## Duplicates & overlaps
- `WatermarkRequest`/`WatermarkPosition` ≡ capture.capnp:203-217 (verbatim).
- `CalibrationStatus` (enum) vs display.capnp:690 (struct) vs sensor.capnp:306 (struct).
- `PrivacyRegion {x, y, width, height :UInt32}` (camera.capnp:274-279) ≡ capture.capnp:184-189 `CaptureRegion` — verbatim rectangle duplicates; `T.Rect` also exists (types.capnp:817).
- `CameraFormat {width, height, pixelFormat, minFps, maxFps, hdr, depthFormat}` vs capture.capnp:115-129 `CaptureFormat {format :T.PixelFormat, width, height, frameRate :T.FrameRate, quality, region, audio, watermark, cursor}` — two format descriptors with different field sets and different frame-rate types (`minFps/maxFps :UInt32` vs `T.FrameRate`).
- `FaceDetection`/`SceneObject` (camera.capnp:373-406) vs capture.capnp:317-347 `CaptureAnalysis` (OCR + objects + faces) — two on-device analysis result shapes.
- `getCalibrationStatus @19` vs display.capnp:201 `getCalibrationStatus @67` and display.capnp:66 `startCalibration` — calibration as a *concept* is forked across three schemas.

## Gaps & holes
- **No frame-delivery path** (above). The fix is small and high-value: `openStream @N (cameraId :T.CameraId, options :VideoOptions) -> (stream :CameraStream)` with `CameraStream.pull (maxBytes :UInt32) -> (frame :CameraFrame)` carrying `pixels :T.DataHandle`, `metadata`, and a monotonic `sequence` — i.e. the ai.capnp:274-279 `ArtifactStream` shape. Gain: a 2046 consumer can get frames; two independent backends interoperate. Cost: ~12 lines and one ordinal.
- **No exposure/focus/zoom range contract.** `setExposure @12 (cameraId, exposure :Float32)` and `setExposureCompensation @13 (cameraId, bias :Float32)` have no units or ranges; `setFocus @11 (cameraId, mode, x :Float32, y :Float32)` has no coordinate space (normalised? sensor pixels? of the preview?). Every implementer guesses, and a client cannot build a UI slider without backend-specific probing.
- **No thermal/limited-mode negotiation.** `setPowerMode @26 (mode :CameraPowerMode {fullQuality, lowPowerPreview, ultraLowPower})` lets a client *request* less quality but there is no way to learn that the device is already throttled, nor an event when throttling begins.
- **`detectFaces @16`/`detectScene @17` are one-shot** — a client tracking faces must poll, and there is no subscription for detection results (contrast input.capnp's event subscription).
- **No multi-camera sync feedback**: `synchronizeCameras @30 (sync :MultiCameraSync)` is fire-and-forget `T.VoidResult`; a client cannot learn whether sync was achieved or drifted.
- **Scanning (the other half of an MFP) has no contract**: `getScanCapability @16` in print.capnp describes a scanner with no corresponding operation. Together these leave the MFP half-modelled in both schemas.

## Zero-cost / eco
- **Correct and worth noting:** `capturePhoto`/`stopRecording` return `T.DataHandle` (camera.capnp:36, 38); `PrivacyMask` regions are computed on the device before capture (camera.capnp:269). Stills-as-blob is the right shape for a photo. The defect is only that *video* and *streams* are missing — see the gap above.
- `startPreview @7` routes to a `T.SurfaceId`, i.e. to the compositor's shared buffer — a genuinely zero-copy path when it is available.
- `detectFaces @16 → List(FaceDetection)` with `landmarks @6 :List(FaceLandmark)` (camera.capnp:373-381) — bounded (≤ ~10 faces × ~70 landmarks) and one-shot; acceptable.
- **Idle-power:** `getRichMetadata @28`, `getCalibrationStatus @19`, `getStabilization @21`, `getNightMode @23`, `getPowerMode @27`, `getRawCaptureOptions @32` are six per-camera getters with no batching and no change events; a settings UI polling them keeps the camera pipeline warm (a camera pipeline is one of the most power-hungry components on a device).

## UX & implementer guidance
- `setFlash @10 (mode :FlashMode)` and `CameraFeature.flash @3`/`torch @4` — flash and torch are the same hardware with different duty cycles; both enums encode them separately with no stated relationship.
- `PhotoOptions.timestamp @5 :T.Timestamp` (camera.capnp:171) puts the capture timestamp in the *request* — i.e. the client chooses when the photo was taken, which then appears in `RichPhotoMetadata`. The camera clock and the client clock can disagree, and nothing says which is authoritative for EXIF.
- `CameraCapabilities.maxResolution @4 :UInt32` (camera.capnp:361) is a single scalar for "maximum resolution" — width×height in what encoding? megapixels? the larger dimension? Unusable for layout.
- `getHardwareInfo @29 → CameraHardwareInfo` with `sensorModel`, `lensModel`, `lensAperture`, `manufacturer` all as `Text` (camera.capnp:512-521) — fine as opaque reporting, but a 2046 event camera or plenoptic array has no lens, so all four become the empty string, and nothing says the empty string means "not applicable" rather than "unknown".
- `SceneDetection.recommendations @3 :List(Text)` (camera.capnp:396) — free-text *recommended camera settings* from the camera server. That is an internal heuristic reported as free text, i.e. a contract no client can implement against (cf. display.capnp's `DisplayRecommendation`).

## Recommended actions
1. **Add a stream contract**: `openStream (cameraId, options) -> (stream :CameraStream)`; `CameraStream.pull (maxBytes) -> (frame :CameraFrame)` with `pixels :T.DataHandle`, `sequence :UInt64`, and pull-backpressure; `cancel`. Gain: the camera becomes usable by headless consumers and two independently-written backends interoperate; activates `routeStream`/`CameraStreamRoute`. Cost: ~14 lines, 3-4 ordinals. **This is the highest-value change in the file.**
2. **Make `stopRecording` chunked** (or return a stream/handle that can be pulled in ranges). Gain: a recording becomes consumable rather than a 22 GB single result. Cost: one signature change plus a range parameter.
3. **Unify the duplicate types**: use `T.Rect` for `PrivacyRegion`, delete the local `WatermarkRequest`/`WatermarkPosition` in favour of capture.capnp's (or promote both to types.capnp), and add one shared `CalibrationStatus`/`SensorCalibration`/`DisplayCalibration` truth. Gain: removes two verbatim duplicates and a same-name-different-kind clash. Cost: 3 type edits.
4. **Replace `PhotoFormat`/`VideoFormat`/`RawColorSpace` with `T.MimeType` + an open `metadata` map.** Gain: new still/video/raw formats need no ordinal, and "raw" stops being a fake format value; separate `RawColorSpace`混乱 collapses into a content type plus reported parameters. Cost: consumers filter by MIME subtype instead of enum.
5. **Add an error union to `capturePhoto`** (and to `stopRecording`). Gain: honest failure instead of an empty `DataHandle`. Cost: one result struct.
6. **Add consent/authority**: gate capture/record/detect behind capability accessors, and align with audio.capnp's mic-consent model so the camera's privacy story is not the missing one of three. Gain: closes the mic/screen/camera privacy asymmetry. Cost: an accessor layer.
7. **Document ranges/units for `setExposure`, `setExposureCompensation`, `setZoom`, `setFocus`'s `x/y`, and `RawCaptureOptions.bitDepth`.** Gain: removes the guessing that every camera backend currently does. Cost: comments — the cheapest high-value fix in the file.
8. Leave `CameraFeature`, `DepthFormat`, `StabilizationMode`, `CameraPowerMode` alone: they have `custom` escapes, and replacing them with open tokens costs consumers the only capability list they have.
