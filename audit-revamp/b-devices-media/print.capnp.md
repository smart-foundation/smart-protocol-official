# audit: `schema/print.capnp` (383 lines)

## Verdict
**KEEP with targeted fixes.** print.capnp is a small, plain contract with the right payload shape (`PrintRequest.source @1 :T.DataHandle` with "use buffer handle for documents >1KB" (print.capnp:186-187), `IccProfile.profileData :T.DataHandle` (349), `print3d`'s payload a `T.DataHandle` (110)) — so R2 is satisfied outright. Typed ids throughout (`T.PrinterId`, `T.PrintJobId`), `T.Pagination` on all lists, `cancel` on the subscription, and `PrintersSubscription.pull (pagination)` acceptably paginated. Three real defects and one scope question. The defects: `PrintTransport {…airprint, mopria, wifidirect…}` is the clearest R5 vendor-noun violation in the batch alongside bluetooth's protocols; `PageSize` carries its width/height **twice** — in inches and in PostScript points — with a hardcoded 1/72 conversion and no rule for which wins; and money is a `Float32` + a bare `Text` currency in two structs while `T.FiatAmount` exists (types.capnp:1372) and its own comment names this exact gap. The scope question is `print3d @28` — an additive machine on a page-printer interface. `DisplayModeFlags` and the unreachable-provider pattern also touch this file's cousin schemas.

## Wire-breakers
- **None.** No compile errors, no ordinal collisions (verified mechanically).
- `print.capnp:145` — `PrinterCapabilities.maxResolution @4 :UInt32` is documented with: "NOTE: Does not distinguish horizontal/vertical DPI. A `T.Resolution` struct would be more precise but is deferred to avoid ordinal changes." **`T.Resolution` already exists** (types.capnp:836-839, with a `ResolutionUnit {dpi, ppi, dpcm, custom}) and types.capnp:826-828 explicitly credits this audit gap. The reason given for not using it ("to avoid ordinal changes") is invalid pre-release and under the doctrine's own "additive only → minor version" rule (DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md:336). A field whose defect is documented, whose fix already exists, and whose only obstacle is an ordinal policy that does not apply yet.

## Duplicates & overlaps
- `IccProfile` (print.capnp:345) vs display.capnp:536 — same name, two types (see R6).
- `PrintColorSpace`/`PrintRenderingIntent` (print.capnp:302-319) vs `T.ColorSpace` (types.capnp:845) vs display.capnp's `ColorSpace`/`RenderingIntent` (383, 550) vs framebuffer's `FramebufferColorSpace`/`ColorManagementIntent` (395, 524) — **four colour-space and four rendering-intent vocabularies in the batch.**
- `Orientation` (print.capnp:221-226) vs `T.SurfaceTransform` (types.capnp:318) vs display.capnp's rotation — a fourth rotation vocabulary.
- `PrintSecurity`/`PrintAccounting`/`PrintQueueStatus` vs capture.capnp's `CapturePermission`/`CaptureAnalysis` and audio.capnp's `MicPolicy` — three "policy/accounting" shapes with no shared model.
- `ConsumableStatus` vs power.capnp's battery/cell monitoring — consumables and batteries are the same concept (a depletable resource with a level and a low threshold) in two schemas.
- `PrintScheduling.quotaGroup @2 :Text` (print.capnp:357) vs `T.Quota` (types.capnp:1776) — a quota is named in two places.

## Gaps & holes
- **No scan contract** (`getScanCapability` with no operation) — the MFP's input half is missing in both print.capnp and capture.capnp.
- **No job-state change events.** There is `PrintersSubscription` (printer arrival/removal) but no `JobSubscription`, so a print-monitor UI must poll `getJobs @7`/`getJob @8` — and `PrintJob.progress @5 :UInt32` (243) is the obvious polling target.
- **No page-count/rollback contract on a failed job**: `PrintJob.pages @4 :UInt32` and `progress @5 :UInt32` exist, but nothing states what `pages` means for a duplex or a cancelled job (pages printed? pages attempted? sheets?).
- **No error detail on a failed job**: `JobState.aborted @5`/`stopped @3` with no `T.Error`, no reason, and no "was paper consumed" signal — a 2046 client cannot tell the user what happened.
- **No media-jam/out-of-media subscription** (consumables are poll-only), and `ConsumableStatus.levelPercent @1 :UInt8` with `isLow @2 :Bool`/`isEmpty @3 :Bool` (361-366) has no threshold definition, so two backends disagree on when `isLow` is true.
- **No default-printer change event** (`setDefaultPrinter @9`/`getDefaultPrinter @10`) — a client cannot learn the default changed except by re-reading.
- **`getPrinter @1 → Printer`** embeds `capabilities :PrinterCapabilities` and `defaults :PrintOptions` (127-128) — so listing printers ships each printer's full capability set and option defaults; fine at 3-4 printers, unbounded at a 2046 print farm without pagination on `listPrinters` (which *is* paginated, so acceptable).

## Zero-cost / eco
- **No inline bulk payloads**: `PrintRequest.source` and `IccProfile.profileData` are `T.DataHandle`, and `print3d`'s payload is a `T.DataHandle`. R2 satisfied — this is one of the three schemas in the batch (with media and font's subset result) that passes cleanly.
- **Polling-forcing:** `getConsumableStatus @27`, `getQueueStatus @12`, `getJob @8`, `getDefaultPrinter @10` are all snapshot getters with no subscription. A cartridge-monitor UI polls a (possibly network) printer continuously — waking the network stack for data that changes over hours. The fix is the schema's own `PrintersSubscription` pattern extended to jobs and consumables.
- `listIccProfiles @23` returns `List(IccProfile)` where each profile carries `profileData :T.DataHandle` (349) — so listing profiles ships every profile's bytes by reference; acceptable, but a client that lists to build a UI pays for handle materialisation it does not need.

## UX / implementer guidance
- `print @2 (printerId, request :PrintRequest)` where `PrintRequest.format @2 :PrintFormat` — the *source* format is a `PrintFormat` enum with `png`/`jpeg` in it, while `PrintRequest.source` is a `T.DataHandle` with no MIME type, so a client cannot tell what bytes it is handing the printer without an enum that does not cover "a PDF with an attached ICC profile".
- `PageSize.name @0 :Text` ("Letter", "A4") has no registry, and `PrintOptions.pageSize @3 :PageSize` inlines a whole `PageSize` (both coordinate systems) rather than referencing one.
- `PrintRange.pages @1 :List(UInt32)` (211) is unbounded — a 2046 job selecting 50,000 individual pages ships a 200 KB list inline.
- `estimateCost @19 (printerId, request :PrintRequest) → PrintCostEstimate` re-submits the entire request just to price it, which for a large document means materialising the source twice.
- `getColorProfile @18 → PrintColorProfile` vs `setColorProfile @17 (profile :PrintColorProfile)` — the getter cannot report a failure, so a query for a printer with no profile returns a default-constructed struct.

## Recommended actions
1. **Replace `Float32`+`Text` money with `T.FiatAmount`** in `PrintAccounting` and `PrintCostEstimate`. Gain: exact currency arithmetic (no float cent errors in a price the user is shown) and one money representation shared with nfc.capnp and any future payments schema. Cost: one type change at two sites.
2. **Collapse `PageSize` to one coordinate system** (inches or points — choose one, or a millimetre-based `T.Size`-style struct) and delete the other pair; document "Backend may round". Gain: removes a two-source-of-truth field where a mismatch silently produces a wrongly-sized page. Cost: two fields deleted (pre-release).
3. **Reduce `PrintTransport` to open tokens or a small spec-closed set** (`local`, `network`, `usb`, `virtual` + `custom :Text`), deleting `airprint`/`mopria`/`wifidirect`. Gain: a 2046 transport needs no ordinal and no product-name commitment. Cost: consumers lose `== PrintTransport.ipp` (they only had it for today's transports).
4. **Add job and consumable subscriptions** (`JobSubscription` with pull+cancel, `ConsumableSubscription`). Gain: a print/cartridge monitor stops polling a network printer; the schema's own `PrintersSubscription` pattern extends with no new machinery. Cost: two small interfaces.
5. **Add `T.Error` to `print`, `schedulePrint`, `uploadIccProfile`, `setColorProfile`, `estimateCost`.** Gain: a failed submission stops returning a job id for a nonexistent job, which is the single most user-visible error path in print. Cost: result structs.
6. **Either give `print3d` its own machine-class contract or scope it out** (a `Print3d` interface with material/slice/queue semantics). Gain: a page printer stops being required to answer `pageSizes` for an additive machine, and a 2046 bioprinter/metal-sintering machine becomes expressible. Cost: one new interface, or the deletion of one method.
7. **Add a scan contract** or delete `getScanCapability`. Gain: the MFP stops advertising an unusable capability. Cost: one method either way.
8. Leave `T.DataHandle` usage, the typed ids, the pagination, `PrinterState`, `JobState`, `DuplexMode` and `MediaType` alone: they are small, defensible, spec- or physics-anchored, and changing them would add machinery without removing any.
